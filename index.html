About

One room, one grill, one long table

Luna Bistro opened in 2019 in a former printworks off Marlowe Street. We kept the brick, the skylight and the enormous communal table the previous owners left behind — everything else we built around a single wood-fired grill.

The menu is written every Tuesday after the market. Some things never leave: the ribeye, the cacio e pepe, the burnt Basque cheesecake. Everything else is a question of what looked good that morning.

Head chef Ines Moreau · Sommelier Tomás Duarte · General manager Rae Salcedo
